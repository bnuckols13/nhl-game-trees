Section 2 · Sentiment — field gravity only

Firewall
Sentiment is an ownership-expectation read and nothing else. It never touches a script probability, a projection, or a capture rate, and nothing in this section recommends changing any number anywhere else in the build. Its only desk use is pricing uniqueness: which of our leaves the field is likely to duplicate, and which it is likely to leave alone. Ownership figures are the vendors' own modeled numbers, cited as theirs.
The gravity center is Colorado and MacKinnon. StatWarrior makes Colorado its top stack and MacKinnon the slate's highest projection, with modeled ownership at 42.5% on the DraftKings field; Fantasy Alarm names him its player of the day; LineStar's FanDuel table is cooler at 10.2%, but the direction never flips anywhere in the sweep. Our Colorado leaves are the most duplicated shapes we own tonight.
