Level 2 · Capture, from the plays

Nashville's power play unit is the most concentrated capture measured in the build: it pays all three participants on 35 of its 68 power play goals, 51%, where the correlation doctrine's other units pay two. When Nashville's power play scores twice, a stack of it does not partially cash; it detonates. The honest limit sits beside it: 14 of those 68 goals paid no member of the unit at all, scored while it was changing or by the second unit. Nashville's assist network is the same story told as pairs, O'Reilly with Forsberg 34 shared goals, Stamkos with O'Reilly 33, Stamkos with Josi 29: five of its six heaviest pairs are power play members paired with each other. That is what a power play team looks like in the record. Carolina inverts the usual pattern: its second line's even-strength share, 21.7%, beats its first line's 19.2%, and pays two members 32 times to the first line's 20. Carolina's even-strength stack is its second line as much as its first.

Kochetkov, named on Carolina's own lineup page and winner of ten straight starts, is a strong favourite goalie at .464, a tier below Tampa's Vasilevskiy (.573) because Nashville shoots and scores enough at home to keep his goals-against term live. Saros carries a correction this file owes the morning sketch, which called him the slate's cleanest goalie-volume script, 35 or more saves in play without needing a win. The simulation agrees on the floor, his save volume is script-independent because Carolina always shoots, and disagrees on the payoff: the joint event the sketch promised, Saros at 20+ in a Nashville loss, prices at 2.1%. Without the 12-point win he needs roughly 33 saves with at most two against, and Carolina's flat scoring makes the two-or-fewer loss the rare version of a Nashville loss. Play Saros as a Nashville goalie, not as a free-standing volume asset. Branches: Annunen for Saros is near-neutral for skaters and moves the slot to .301, but it voids the Saros sprinkle below; Bussi for Kochetkov adds about 0.6 goals to Nashville's mean and fattens every Nashville leaf while the Kochetkov leaves die. Saros versus Annunen is the game's biggest T-60 item, because this tree's leaf structure leans on the crease more than most.
