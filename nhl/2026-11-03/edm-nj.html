Level 2 · Capture, from the plays

Edmonton's network is a triangle, not a star: McDavid and Bouchard share 78 goals, Draisaitl and McDavid 73, Draisaitl and Bouchard 51, and no other pair on either roster in today's games is close. The three are one asset for capture purposes, at even strength and on the power play alike, and splitting them across lineups splits the asset. The listed first line pays two members on 41 of 226 even-strength goals, 18%, the strongest even-strength line correlation measured today, and it is entirely the McDavid-Draisaitl pairing. New Jersey runs the other way: its first line is the decoy, holding 15.4% of even-strength points, while its second line, Gritsyuk, Hughes and Bratt, holds 28.0% and pays two members on 32 of 183 goals. Bratt and Jack Hughes, 48 shared goals, are the pair the whole New Jersey side runs through.

Both power plays pay three participants nearly every time they score, Edmonton 94% of the time, New Jersey 98%, so the correlation is real on both sides; the difference is volume, an expected 0.80 power play goals for Edmonton against 0.55 for New Jersey. Edmonton's unit takes 79.7% of the points on its power play goals, but the capture is four men, not five: McDavid participates in 82.1% of power play goals in his dressed games, Draisaitl 68.9%, Bouchard 47.6%, Nugent-Hopkins 44.6%, and Kapanen 9.1%, four points on 44 team power play goals. His seat is an announcement, not a record, the same pattern as Vancouver's Lekkerimaki and one step worse, and the cap math drops him first. New Jersey's unit share, 64.0%, is the lowest of the four units priced today, hub-shaped through Jack Hughes at 60.5% participation, with its own unproven fifth seat in Evangelista, six dressed power play goals in two seasons. Hamilton, who is not on the listed power play unit at all, out-captures Luke Hughes in the record and is Jack Hughes's third-largest partner: a defense-led New Jersey boom runs through Hamilton at $4,500, not through the point man, and the point seat itself is contested until warmups.
