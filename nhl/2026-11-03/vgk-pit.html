The news that reprices the skaters

Malkin is out, settled on the league's own status report, and the morning build had him projected in. Karlsson holds 16 participation points, shares five goals each with Rakell and Crosby, and runs the power play hub, four shared power play goals with Novak. Novak, who moves up to Crosby's first-line wing, is the one Pittsburgh skater whose measured rates understate him, because they were built in the smaller job he had yesterday.
